Smart Helmet: Control Room, First Version

A wireframe of the first version of our software. A rough layout to agree on before we build.

Prepared for Thella Group Services and Imperium Corporate Prepared by 90Ten Group 20 October 2026 Version 1

What the first version will do

One goal: show that a helmet can report to our own server and that the control room can see where a worker was and when.

It will show
  • Which helmets are online
  • Where each worker was last seen
  • The trail of beacons they passed with the time
  • Alarms such as SOS or a fall
  • The battery level
It will use
  • A sample helmet that reports to our server
  • The helmet's own report: battery and the beacons it hears with the time
  • A name we give each beacon such as "Level 2, west tunnel"
Not in this version
  • Video and photos
  • Voice calls
  • Sending updates to helmets
  • Reports and many mines

How to read the drawings

These are rough layouts only. They show what goes where and not the final look. Names and times are sample data. Numbers in black circles are explained under each drawing.

Screen 1: Overview

The first thing the control room sees. It answers: is everyone fine and what needs attention?

  1. Four numbers at the top. A glance shows if anything is wrong.
  2. The mine layout with levels. Each filled circle is a helmet and a ring is an alarm. The layout is drawn once for each mine.
  3. The alarms list. The newest alarm is at the top. Tapping one opens screen 3.

Screen 2: One worker

Pick a worker to see where they have been today. This is the trail we get from the beacons.

  1. A list of workers with a search box. A filled dot means the helmet is online. An empty dot means it has not been seen.
  2. The trail. Each line is a beacon the worker passed with the time. The last line is where they were last seen.
  3. A grey tag means the helmet saved this while it had no signal and sent it later. This is the "save and send later" idea. It depends on question 5.
  4. The same trail drawn on the mine layout.

Screen 3: Alarm and missing person

What opens when an alarm comes in. It is also what the control room uses to find someone who has not come out.

  1. The alarm bar. Who it is and where they were last seen. Two buttons: acknowledge or open the trail.
  2. The last seen card. The place and the time the helmet last reported and when the signal was lost.
  3. Who was near the same beacon in the last half hour. They can be called first. The danger zone check shows if the worker was in the face zone near blast time. It depends on question 7.
  4. The way the worker came on the mine layout. The search at the bottom finds anyone by name.

Screen 4: Setup

Where we tell the system what each beacon is. It is done once for each mine.

  1. Three tabs: beacons and helmets and the server. The server tab shows the address the helmets report to, which is our own.
  2. The beacon list. Each beacon has an address that the helmet reports. We give it a name and say which level and area it is in.
  3. New beacons that helmets hear appear here with a button to name them. A walk through the mine finds them all. Phones and speakers show up too and can be ignored.
What this first version depends on

A sample helmet and its admin login on the supplier's platform so we can point it to our server. The answers to questions 1 and 4 on beacons. Question 3 on the map. Everything else in this document can be built from the Interface Document.
