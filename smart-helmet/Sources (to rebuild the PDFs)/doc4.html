Smart Helmet: Critical Questions, Set 2

Certificates, repairs, the lamp clip, the camera and video, the battery and Wi-Fi.

Prepared for Thella Group Services and Imperium Corporate Prepared by 90Ten Group 20 October 2026 Version 1

The next questions

No.QuestionWhy we ask
1Which certificates does the helmet have for South Africa? Can you send us a copy of each?Mines will ask for proof before any helmet goes underground. We have seen none yet.
2If a helmet breaks, who can repair it in South Africa? Are spare parts sold here and can we have a repair guide and the warranty terms?The documents show no repair or parts information.
3Can a mine's own lamp be fixed to the helmet? Is there a clip, what does it cost to make one and does the helmet have its own light?Mines already own lamps. We want to keep them.
4How well does the camera see in the dark? Can the helmet send video while an SOS call is open?The control room needs to see what is happening at the time of an alarm.
5How many hours does the battery last with the camera on? How long can the memory card record?A shift is long. The helmet must last it.
6Can the helmet join Wi-Fi points on its own, remember several and work on Wi-Fi alone with no SIM card?Some mines have no Wi-Fi and some have it only in a few places.
