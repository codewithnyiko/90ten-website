What we have heard from Imperium

The documents

They were compiled and reviewed with the supplier because the supplier did not have one single document with all the necessary information.

The certificates

Imperium proposed keeping the hardware certificates separate for now so we stay focused on the software. We agreed and asked that they stay in view because no mine will accept the helmet without them.

The prototype

Imperium asked how soon we can have a prototype of the software. Our answer depends on when we get a sample helmet and its admin login.
