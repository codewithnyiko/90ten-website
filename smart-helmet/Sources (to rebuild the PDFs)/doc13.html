What runs on our own servers

Once a helmet is told our address it talks only to us. These are the parts we run in South Africa. The supplier's servers are not needed after that.

PartWhat it doesWhen
Address serverTells each helmet where to report. One small web page (pp3 and 4).Version 1
Live serverKeeps the live connection with every helmet. Takes the login and the reports and the alarms and sends commands back (pp10 to 21).Version 1
Data storeKeeps every report and alarm so the control room can show history.Version 1

File serverReceives the photos and audio and video that helmets upload (pp22 to 34).Version 3
Video serverReceives live video from a helmet (p37).Version 4
Call serverLets the control room talk to a helmet on a video call (p39).Version 4
One thing to add ourselves

The helmet logs in with its device number only and there is no password (p10). Our live server will keep a list of allowed device numbers and refuse the rest.
