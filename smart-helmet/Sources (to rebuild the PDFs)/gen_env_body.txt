<h2>What the existing environment is</h2>
<p class="lead">v1.00 does not change the helmet. It uses what is already there and changes only where the helmet sends its reports.</p>
<table class="q"><thead><tr><th>Part</th><th>Who has it</th><th>What we do with it</th></tr></thead><tr><td class="qq"><b>The helmet</b></td><td>The supplier made it. It has a SIM card for 4G.</td><td>Nothing. It stays as it is.</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>The address server</b></td><td>The supplier. It sits at hm.equiinet.cn and is built into the helmet (p3).</td><td>Nothing. The helmet still asks it where to report.</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>The supplier platform</b></td><td>The supplier. We get an admin login for our helmets.</td><td>We log in once and change the two addresses to ours (p3).</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>The mobile network</b></td><td>The SIM card on each helmet.</td><td>Nothing. The helmet reports over 4G on the surface.</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>Our servers</b></td><td>Ours. They run in South Africa.</td><td>They receive the reports and keep them and show them.</td></tr></table>
<div class="box dark"><b>What changes and what does not</b><p>What changes is the two addresses the helmet is told to report to. What does not change is the helmet itself and its software and the supplier address server it asks first.</p></div>

<h2>How a helmet reaches our server</h2>
<p class="lead">The helmet first asks the supplier's address server where to report. The answer is our server. From then on the reports go to us.</p>

<div class="pg">
<h2>A shift from start to end</h2>
<p class="lead">What happens in v1.00 during one shift for one worker.</p>
<table class="q"><thead><tr><th>When</th><th>What happens</th><th>Where</th></tr></thead><tr><td class="qq"><b>Before the shift</b></td><td>At the lamp room the worker is linked to a helmet and a shift. The screen shows if the helmet is seen and its battery.</td><td>Our sign in screen</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>Shift starts</b></td><td>The helmet is switched on and asks where to report and logs in to our server.</td><td>Helmet and our live server</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>During the shift</b></td><td>The helmet reports every 10 seconds. The control room sees the worker on the map and who is seen and who is not.</td><td>Our overview and shift view</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>An alarm</b></td><td>A fall or an SOS reaches our server at once. The control room sees who and where and when. A video call needs a call server so it comes in a later version.</td><td>Our alarm screen</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>A headcount</b></td><td>The control room lists everyone signed in by where they were last seen and ticks people safe.</td><td>Our headcount screen</td></tr><tr><td class="qq"><b>Shift ends</b></td><td>The worker hands the helmet back and it is returned in the sign in screen. The history keeps who had it.</td><td>Our sign in screen</td></tr></table>
<div class="box"><b>Not in v1.00</b><p>The underground trail and data saved with no signal and fences and live video and video calls. They come in later versions.</p></div>
