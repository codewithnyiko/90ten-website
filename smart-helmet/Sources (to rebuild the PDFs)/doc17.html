Smart Helmet: How v1.00 Works in the Existing Environment

How the application works while the helmets still use the supplier's existing servers.

Prepared for Thella Group Services and Imperium Corporate Designed and prepared by 90Ten Group 20 October 2026 v1.00

What the existing environment is

v1.00 does not change the helmet. It uses what is already there and changes only where the helmet sends its reports. Message formats follow the supplier's Interface Document for Device-side Connecting.

PartWho has itWhat we do with it
The helmetThe supplier made it. It has a SIM card for 4G.Nothing. It stays as it is.
The address serverThe supplier. It sits at hm.equiinet.cn and is built into the helmet.Nothing. The helmet still asks it where to report.
The supplier platformThe supplier. We get an admin login for the helmets.We log in once and change the two addresses to the client's server.
The mobile networkThe SIM card on each helmet.Nothing. The helmet reports over 4G on the surface.
The client's serversThe client's. They run in South Africa.They receive the reports and keep them and show them.
What changes and what does not

What changes is the two addresses the helmet is told to report to. What does not change is the helmet itself and its software and the supplier address server it asks first.

How a helmet reaches the client's server

The helmet first asks the supplier's address server where to report. The answer is the client's server. From then on the reports go there.

No.What happens
1The helmet switches on and asks the address server where to report.
2The address server answers with the two addresses we set. They point to the client's server.
3The helmet opens a live link to the live server and logs in with its device number.
4It sends a report every 10 seconds with its position and battery and the Bluetooth devices it hears.
5If an alarm happens it sends the alarm at once.
6The data store keeps every report and the screens show it.

The data we get from the helmet

The helmet keeps a live link open and sends its data to us. We do not have to ask for it. A report arrives every 10 seconds.

Hosting

v1.00 is built and tested on a 90Ten test server with sample data. It is then installed on the client's own servers in South Africa and the data stays there.

A shift from start to end

What happens in v1.00 during one shift for one worker.

WhenWhat happensWhere
Before the shiftAt the lamp room the worker is linked to a helmet and a shift. The screen shows if the helmet is seen and its battery.Sign in screen
Shift startsThe helmet is switched on and asks where to report and logs in to the client server.Helmet and live server
During the shiftThe helmet reports every 10 seconds. The control room sees the worker on the map and who is seen and who is not.Overview and shift view
An alarmA fall or an SOS reaches the server at once. The control room sees who and where and when. A video call needs a call server so it comes in a later version.Alarm screen
A headcountThe control room lists everyone signed in by where they were last seen and ticks people safe.Headcount screen
Shift endsThe worker hands the helmet back and it is returned in the sign in screen. The history keeps who had it.Sign in screen
Not in v1.00

The underground trail and data saved with no signal and fences and live video and video calls. They come in later versions.
