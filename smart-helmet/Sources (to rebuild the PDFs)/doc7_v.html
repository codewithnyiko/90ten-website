Smart Helmet: Hardware Questions for Later

Certificates, repairs, the lamp clip, the camera and the battery. To follow once the software is working.

Prepared for Thella Group Services and Imperium Corporate Prepared by 90Ten Group 20 October 2026 v1.00

The hardware questions

No.QuestionWhy we ask
1Which certificates does the helmet have for South Africa? Can you send us a copy of each?Mines will ask for proof before any helmet goes underground. We have seen none yet.
2The helmet is made abroad. Can the supplier send spare parts such as a camera that is broken or not clear, a battery or a shell? Can a local technician fit them? Can we have a repair guide and the warranty terms?We cannot send a helmet overseas every time something breaks. The documents show no repair or parts information.
3Can a mine's own lamp be fixed to the helmet? Is there a clip and what does it cost to make one? Does the helmet have its own light?Mines already own lamps. We want to keep them.
4How well does the camera see in the dark?The picture must be clear enough to see what is happening.
5How many hours does the battery last with the camera on? How long can the memory card record?A shift is long. The helmet must last it.
